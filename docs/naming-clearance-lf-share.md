## TL;DR

We're naming an **OpenMobileHub** open-source library that makes an AI agent **prove a verifiable credential

it always appears namespaced `@openmobilehub/…` and foundation-branded, and "MCP" qualifies it as MCP-native.
**We're asking for your sign-off to proceed** — and a flag if you see a problem.

## The one thing to be aware of

The *bare* working name "Attesto" had a same-category lookalike — **attesto.dev** (Dimenfinity; hardware-signed
access security, owns the `.dev`), plus **Attesto Inc.** (attesto.com; identity-fraud) and an "Attesso"
homophone. **AttestoMCP is our move to distinguish from these** — distinct string, namespaced, MCP-qualified. The
only open question is whether keeping "Attesto" as the root is **sufficiently distinguished** — your call. (No
registered "ATTESTO" mark surfaced in USPTO/Justia/Trademarkia; EUIPO/TMview not searched at record level.)
